About Whitewater

Whitewater Capital is a private investment firm pursuing opportunistic, event-driven investments across private markets.

Targeting special situations created by dislocation, complexity and evolving capital needs, we invest directly in real estate and private companies, co-invest alongside established sponsors and selectively invest in differentiated funds.

We leverage our institutional investment experience, deep relationships and flexible mandate to identify and provide our investors access to compelling opportunities across sectors and throughout the capital stack.

Whitewater was founded by Wesley Simons, Managing Partner, who brings more than 11 years of institutional principal investing experience across real estate and private equity.
