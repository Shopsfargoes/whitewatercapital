About Whitewater

Whitewater Capital is a private investment firm focused on opportunistic investments across real estate and private equity.

Targeting special situations arising from dislocation, complexity and evolving capital needs, we invest primarily in real estate assets, related operating platforms and private companies, co-invest alongside established sponsors and selectively invest in differentiated funds.

We leverage our institutional investment experience, deep relationships and flexible mandate to provide our investors access to high-conviction opportunities across sectors, geographies and transaction structures.

Whitewater was founded by Wesley Simons, who serves as Managing Partner and brings more than 11 years of institutional investing experience spanning over $2 billion in aggregate transaction volume at prior firms.
